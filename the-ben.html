The ancestor

Benjamin Franklin carried a notebook everywhere he went. In it he tracked thirteen virtues and asked himself two questions every day.

Morning

"What good shall I do today?"

Evening

"What good have I done today?"

The notebook was not for remembering. It was for becoming. It closed the distance between the person he intended to be and the person he actually was, one page at a time.
